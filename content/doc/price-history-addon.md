**Display property price history with beautiful analytics and automated government reporting!**

The **Price History Add-on** for [Interactive Real Estate](https://wordpress.org/plugins/interactive-real-estate/) is the essential bridge between your property listings and the transparency modern buyers—and regulators—demand. Whether you are aiming to comply with new EU consumer protection laws or simply want to give your buyers more data to feel confident, this add-on is your solution.

---

## Three Reasons Why Your Add-on is a Must-Have:

![create floor](/assets/doc/price-history-gif.gif)

1. **Guarantees Legal Compliance & Protection**
   Designed specifically to meet the strict requirements of real estate laws (like those recently introduced in Poland). It automatically logs every price change with a timestamp and generates the necessary daily XML reports for government portals like _dane.gov.pl_, protecting you from heavy fines.

2. **Builds Instant Buyer Trust**
   Transparency sells. By showing historical price trends directly on the interactive map, you provide buyers with the data they need to feel confident. Showing a "price reduced" history or a steady value increase helps create urgency and trust with potential investors.

3. **Eliminates Manual Data Management**
   Instead of manually updating spreadsheets or government portals, the add-on handles the logging. Every time you change a price in your IRE dashboard, the add-on silently handles the database logging, updates the front-end charts, and prepares the data for external exports.
